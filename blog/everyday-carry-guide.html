This is an editorial category guide, not a claim that every reader needs all 25 items. Compare each category with the routines, constraints and rules that apply to daily work and travel.

1. Slim Card Wallet

A slim card wallet replaces the bulky bifold that pushes on your hip all day. Aim for four to eight cards plus a few folded bills, and look for RFID-blocking metal or tight leather that keeps cards from sliding out. If you carry more than eight cards, trim before you buy.

How to choose the final setup

Review the carry after several weeks. Remove unused items, maintain refillable tools and keep a consistent location for each essential. A smaller, repeatable system is usually easier to trust.
