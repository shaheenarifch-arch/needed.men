Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Needed.Men ("we", "us") collects when you visit needed.men, how it is used and the choices you have. We keep data collection to a minimum.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter sign-ups. If you subscribe, we collect the email address you enter. It is used only to send occasional field guides and updates. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, or you can ask us to remove you.
  • Messages you send. If you email or call us, we keep your message and contact details to respond.
  • Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and understand which guides are useful. It is privacy-first: it does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not build a profile of you.

Cookies and affiliate links

Needed.Men itself does not set advertising cookies. However, when you click an affiliate link and visit a retailer, that retailer or affiliate network may set cookies so the purchase can be credited to us. This includes:

  • Amazon. Needed.Men is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. Third parties, including Amazon, may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, to collect information when you click an Amazon link. See the Amazon Privacy Notice.
  • Awin. Partner store links tracked through Awin may set a tracking cookie. See the Awin privacy policy.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not stop the site from working.

How we use and share information

We use the information above to run the site, send the newsletter you asked for and reply to you. We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the site (hosting, analytics and email delivery), or when required by law.

Retention and security

Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Messages are kept as long as needed to handle your request. We use HTTPS across the site and limit access to the information we hold.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California and the EU/UK, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to opt out of its sale (we do not sell it). To make a request, email agents@getservices.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Questions? Contact us.
